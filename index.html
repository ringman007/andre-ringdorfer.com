How I work

  • Spec first, then agents. I write the requirements and the checks, and AI coding agents build against them. If a placeholder is left unfinished, the build fails.
  • Research in days, not weeks. Competitive analyses, data catalogues and market studies. For one, I mapped 538 data feeds and graded how much of each was really proprietary.
  • Reading the chain directly. When a stablecoin slips off its peg, I go to the raw onchain data to find out who holds it and why.
